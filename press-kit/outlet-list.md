# Where to send it — outlet list & tips

> ⚠️ Submission addresses change. **Always verify on the outlet's current "Contact" / "Submit" page** before sending. The addresses below are starting points, not guarantees.

## Tier 1 — best fit (free / open-source friendly)

### Bedroom Producers Blog (BPB) ← primary target
- Look for their submission/tips address on the site footer or "Contact" page (historically a `tips@` style address, plus a contact form).
- They explicitly cover free plugins. Your all-free, open-source angle is ideal.
- They love a human story. Lead with "self-taught hobbyist rebuilt reverb from papers."

### Synth Anatomy
- Covers free + paid, news-style. Has a contact form / email on site.
- Likes novel DSP and indie devs.

### rekkerd.org
- Long-running plugin news aggregator. Submit via their contact page.
- Good for straightforward "new free plugin released" news.

## Tier 2 — communities (post yourself, follow each board's self-promo rules)

### KVR Audio
- Forums: "Instruments" / "Effects" / product announcements. Also a product database you can list Ambience in (free).
- Huge, active, the canonical plugin community.

### Reddit
- r/edmproduction (check the self-promo / "Feedback Friday" rules)
- r/WeAreTheMusicMakers
- r/audioengineering
- Post the before/after video + a short story. Don't just drop a link.

### VI-Control, Gearspace (forums)
- Niche but serious audio crowds.

## Tier 3 — video / social reach
- Tag relevant **YouTube reviewers** of free plugins in a reply or short DM with the demo link.
- Short-form: a 30–60s vertical cut of the before/after demo for X / Reels / Shorts.

## Submission tips (apply to all)
1. **One plugin per email.** Ambience only.
2. **Subject line = the hook.** "Free, open-source algorithmic reverb (16-channel FDN)."
3. **First sentence states what it is + that it's free.** Editors skim.
4. **Attach 2–3 images max**, or just link the feature page (it has everything).
5. **Offer, don't demand:** "Happy to send audio samples / more shots."
6. **One follow-up only**, after 1–2 weeks (see followup-EN.md).
7. **When featured:** thank them publicly, share the article, and capture the traffic spike (pin a post, have your newsletter/X ready).

## Pre-send checklist
- [ ] GitHub Release has a clearly named, downloadable `.vst3` (or installer/zip)
- [ ] Feature page loads on mobile
- [ ] Demo video is public (not unlisted) if you want it embedded
- [ ] Your X / note links work
- [ ] You verified the outlet's current contact address
