Hi BPB team,

I'm a solo, self-taught developer behind **OTODESK**, where I build free and open-source VST3 plugins. I'd love to share my latest release — I think it's a strong fit for BPB's free-plugin coverage.

**Ambience** is a research-grade algorithmic reverb built on a 16-channel Feedback Delay Network. Highlights:

- 7 reverb algorithms (ROOM / HALL / PLATE / SPRING / GOLDFOIL)
- 21 factory presets modeled after real, world-class spaces — Abbey Road, Vienna Musikverein, Concertgebouw, Carnegie Hall, EMT 140, and more
- A Välimäki–Liski 10-band GEQ absorption stage for frequency-dependent RT60
- ISM-based early reflections, an ADAA saturator (Warm / Tape / Tube / Hard), and live D50 / C50 / C80 / EDT acoustic readouts

It's **completely free and open-source (GPLv3)**.

A bit of context on why I made it: I'm not a trained audio engineer — just a hobbyist producer who got frustrated that most commercial reverbs still rest on 1960s foundations (Schroeder, Moorer, Jot) and sell mostly on presets and UI. So I rebuilt the fundamentals from academic papers, with AI as a sparring partner, one stubborn build error at a time. Ambience is the result. Over the past year I've shipped 12 plugins this way — all free, all open-source.
